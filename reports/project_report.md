# AI-Powered Business Intelligence and Sales Forecasting Dashboard

## 1. Abstract

This project develops an interactive Business Intelligence Dashboard using Python and Streamlit. It allows users to upload business datasets, clean data, analyze sales performance, visualize business trends, generate insights, and forecast future monthly sales using Machine Learning.

## 2. Problem Statement

Businesses often work with large datasets and need an efficient way to understand sales, revenue, profit, and product performance. Manual analysis can take time and make it difficult to identify trends.

This project provides an interactive dashboard to simplify business data analysis and support data-driven decision-making.

## 3. Objectives

- Upload CSV and Excel datasets.
- Identify missing values and duplicate records.
- Clean and prepare business data.
- Calculate important business metrics.
- Visualize sales and profit performance.
- Generate rule-based business insights.
- Predict future monthly sales using Linear Regression.
- Download analysis results and forecasts.

## 5. Project Modules

### Module 1: Data Upload
Supports CSV and Excel file uploads.

### Module 2: Data Cleaning
Identifies missing values, duplicate rows, and data types. Provides options to clean the dataset.

### Module 3: Business Analytics
Calculates total revenue, total profit, average sales, and transaction counts.

### Module 4: Data Visualization
Displays interactive product comparisons, revenue contribution, monthly sales trends, profit distribution, and revenue-versus-profit charts.

### Module 5: Business Insights
Uses predefined business rules to identify high-performing products, low-performing products, and sales trends.

### Module 6: Machine Learning Predictions
Uses Linear Regression to estimate future monthly sales based on historical monthly sales data.

### Module 7: Reports
Allows users to download cleaned datasets, business insight reports, and future sales forecasts.

## 6. Machine Learning Methodology

The project uses Linear Regression for monthly sales forecasting.

1. Select a date column and sales column.
2. Convert transaction dates into monthly periods.
3. Aggregate sales for each month.
4. Create sequential time-based input features.
5. Split historical data chronologically into training and testing sets.
6. Train the Linear Regression model.
7. Evaluate predictions using Mean Absolute Error (MAE) and R² where available.
8. Retrain using all historical data and forecast future months.

## 7. Expected Outcomes

The dashboard is designed to help users:

- Understand business performance.
- Identify product-level sales trends.
- Examine revenue and profit.
- Explore historical sales patterns.
- Obtain estimated future sales.
- Export useful business reports.

## 8. Limitations

- Forecast accuracy depends on the quality and amount of historical data.
- At least five months of valid sales history are required.
- Linear Regression models a simple trend and does not explicitly account for seasonality.
- Business insights are rule-based rather than generated by a trained AI language model.
- Predictions are estimates, not guaranteed future results.
